The whole type scale in Outfit, each size annotated with its height in arcminutes at 2.25 m and the furthest distance it stays legible.

Check this on the panel itself, not a laptop. Serve it from the Pi, sit where you normally sit, and read down until a line stops being legible. That line is your real floor; if it falls above `stat-lg`, the scale needs re-deriving from `legibility.md`.

Numbers are measured on digit height (72.5% of the font size in Outfit), uppercase labels on capital height (70.5%), and mixed-case words on x-height (48.5%), because that is what the eye uses for each.

The panel between the tiers marks 16 arcminutes (97px of font size), ISO 9241-303's minimum. Sizes below it are deliberate: recognised by shape, or for walking closer.

The kit page scales previews to fit its own width, so sizes here are proportionally right but not physically exact. For a true-size check, open it at 1024px wide.
